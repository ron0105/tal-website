## The challenge

Alkaline water was a category most Indian buyers had never heard of. The content had to explain the product, make bottled water feel like a lifestyle choice, and give the page a personality in a crowded wellness feed.

## The work

Every post had one of three jobs.

- **Explain the product.** Simple visuals that put the benefits in one picture, so the category was easy to grasp.
- **Sell the lifestyle.** Posts that tied hydration to an active life, backed by collaborations with fitness and outdoor influencers.
- **Keep it human.** Light, shareable posts with no sales pitch, like an angry burger holding "pH level high high!" signs.

Alongside the posts: the copy, the content plan, blog articles and campaign content, made with the design team.

## What it did

Over a year, the page grew steadily in followers, engagement and reach. The brand found its own place with its audience, and sales grew alongside it.

## What this looks like for you

If people don't yet know why they need what you sell, the same three pillars work: one post type that explains, one that shows the life it fits into, and one that's simply fun to share.
