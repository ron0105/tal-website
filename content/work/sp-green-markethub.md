## The challenge

A growing brand was selling fans and water heaters across Amazon, Flipkart and quick-commerce apps, from four warehouses in Bhiwandi, Delhi, Hyderabad and Kolkata. Orders slipped, stock sat in the wrong city, and every week went into matching spreadsheets by hand.

## The work

- **Dispatch and receipt, end to end.** One workflow across Amazon, Flipkart and the other platforms, with dispatch planned before the slot is booked.
- **New marketplaces, opened properly.** Flipkart, Amazon, Retailez, Zepto, Homerun and Fixxly, each with a working order-to-payment pipeline.
- **A live stock and order tracker.** One Excel system across four warehouses and the Amazon and Flipkart order portals, so nobody reconciles by hand.
- **Smarter stock moves.** A matching method that sends surplus stock from one warehouse to the region that's short, across about 43 products.
- **Space and cash back.** Old stock and scrap cleared, and a 5S clean-up that freed warehouse floor in every city.
- **A new 13,000 sq ft warehouse,** planned with racking sized for the full range.

## What this looks like for you

Orders that leave on time, stock where your customers are, and an end to the weekly spreadsheet hunt.
