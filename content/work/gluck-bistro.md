## The challenge

A new, founder-led bistro had a strong identity but no clear path from "people like the place" to "people find it, come back and bring friends", or a clear view of whether each customer was profitable.

## The work

- **Mapped the customer journey.** Who the bistro is for, how they discover it, and what turns a first visit into a regular.
- **Wrote the brand story.** Positioning and visual content ideas that carry it.
- **Planned 90 days.** Social content, reviews, referrals and loyalty, in order, each feeding the next.
- **Made the numbers simple.** A guide to orders, cost to win a customer, repeat visits and profit per order.
- **Made the materials.** Campaign posters and QR cards that send diners to reviews and offers.

## What this looks like for you

Posting more isn't a growth plan. One that works links where people find you, why they come back, and what each customer is worth, so you know which step to fix first.
