## The challenge

Give an AI accessibility platform a clear, believable voice in a B2B market that doubts both AI and quick-fix accessibility tools.

## The work

- **Positioning built on two ideas.** A scan sees your website, Pluro sees your users. And AI that helps teams work smarter, not AI that replaces them.
- **Content with a creative team.** Carousels, posts and reels in a problem-then-solution, fact-led format.
- **Weekly long-form blogs** for the website.
- **Lead generation** through LinkedIn outreach and engagement, plus ghostwritten founder posts.
- **Clear audiences:** agencies, business owners, accessibility teams, product and UX teams, developers and compliance leads.
