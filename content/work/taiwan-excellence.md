## The challenge

Taiwan Excellence speaks for a group of Taiwanese tech brands in India. The job was to make those brands matter to Indian buyers, by linking them to what India already loves: cricket, gaming and pop culture.

## The work

- **Taiwan Excellence Gaming Cup.** A nationwide eSports tournament, written, pitched and run three years in a row: the campaign idea, the copy, the social posts and the paid ads.
- **Mumbai Indians × Taiwan Excellence.** An IPL sponsorship turned into fan-first campaigns, on the ground and online. #GullyCricketRules asked fans to share the rules of their own street cricket.
- **One voice for nine brands.** Launch and always-on campaigns that kept each brand distinct under one banner.
