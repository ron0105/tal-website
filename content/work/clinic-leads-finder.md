## The problem

Clinics lose patient enquiries in places owners never look: no website on their Google listing, a broken link, a message that waits a day for a reply.

## What it does

Give it a clinic type and an area. It pulls the public Google listings, opens each clinic's website, and flags the gaps that lose enquiries. Then it drafts one short WhatsApp message per clinic, so the owner hears about the gap from someone who checked.

## The first run, October 2026

We checked 30 dentists with 50 or more Google reviews. Two in three had a gap. A third had no website on Google at all. One clinic's website link opened a gambling site. And almost all the clinics with a gap were rated 4.8 or higher: good clinics, losing patients online.

## What this looks like for you

A clear list of what's costing you enquiries today, and the fix for each one.
