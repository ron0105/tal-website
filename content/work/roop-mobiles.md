## The challenge

A growing retailer of phones, TVs, ACs and home appliances had no clear brand and no online presence. It needed content that spoke to customers in a tier-2 city, in their own words.

## The work, over six months

- A brand identity from scratch: tone of voice, colours, type and visual style
- Daily local content and ad copy, in Hindi and English, built around festivals, launches and easy-finance offers
- Social media across Facebook, Instagram and X
- Meta and Google ads aimed at one thing: getting people into the stores
