## The challenge

The business needed a more structured way to track customers and enquiries, and wanted to use AI in daily work without adding complexity.

## The work

- **Requirements first.** Turned what the team needed into a short list of CRM priorities.
- **Set up Zoho CRM.** Configured it around their real sales and customer process.
- **Added AI where it helps.** Built AI into everyday workflows inside the CRM.
- **Explained it.** Walked the team through how it works and what to automate next.

## What it did

The client confirmed in a letter that the CRM and AI work was delivered, and recommended the same work to others.

## What this looks like for you

If your leads live in WhatsApp chats, notebooks and memory, a CRM set up around how you already work means no enquiry gets lost, and follow-ups happen on time.
