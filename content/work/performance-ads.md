## The challenge

Make ads people don't skip. On Instagram, a polished ad looks like an ad, and gets scrolled past.

## The work

Three short videos. Two are creator-style ads, where a person talks to the camera the way a friend would. One is a customer-style video (often called UGC) that starts from a real problem.
