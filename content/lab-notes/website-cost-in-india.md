**Most small business websites in India cost between ₹10,000 and ₹1,50,000 to build, plus a few thousand rupees a year for the domain and hosting. Online stores usually cost ₹1,50,000 to ₹4,00,000 or more with an agency.** The price moves with three things: how many pages and features you need, who builds it, and whether the price includes the words, photos and setup that make it bring in enquiries.

## Typical prices in 2026

| What you're building | Typical price | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Template site you set up yourself | Under ₹10,000 | Getting online fast, with limited design |
| Small business site, 5 to 15 pages | ₹10,000 to ₹80,000 | Clinics, salons, consultants, local services |
| Custom business site, 10 to 20 pages | ₹80,000 to ₹1,50,000 | Firms that need a unique design or booking |
| Online store (agency) | ₹1,50,000 to ₹4,00,000+ | Brands selling products online |
| Online store (freelancer, Shopify or WooCommerce) | ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 | A simple store with a ready theme |
| Domain and hosting, every year | A few thousand rupees | Every site |

These ranges come from Indian web hosts and agencies (sources at the end). They vary a lot, so treat them as a guide and compare two or three real quotes.

## What changes the price

- **Pages.** Ten pages cost more than five, but a clear five-page site often beats a messy twenty-page one.
- **Features.** Booking, payments, WhatsApp buttons, forms that send to your phone, and a blog each add work.
- **Words and photos.** Many quotes assume you'll write the content and send the photos. Writing that brings in enquiries is real work, so check if it's included.
- **Who builds it.** Freelancers usually cost less. Agencies usually cost more but bring design, writing and project management.
- **Setup for Google.** Page titles, a sitemap, speed, and linking your Google Business Profile decide whether people ever find the site.
- **After launch.** Updates, fixes and backups, either included for a while or charged monthly.

## What you should get for the money

Whatever you pay, a business website in 2026 should:

1. Look right and load fast on a phone, because that's where most people will see it.
2. Have a WhatsApp or call button that works in one tap.
3. Send form enquiries straight to you, not to an inbox nobody checks.
4. Have a clear title and description on every page, and a sitemap for Google.
5. Link to your Google Business Profile, with the same name, address and phone.
6. Be on a domain **you** own, in your name, with the login shared with you.

## Costs people forget

- **Renewals.** Domains and hosting renew every year, often at a higher price than the first year.
- **Paid plugins and themes.** Some charge a yearly fee.
- **Email.** A professional email on your domain is usually a separate monthly cost.
- **Changes later.** Ask what a small change costs after launch, before you sign.

## Common questions

**Is a ₹5,000 website worth it?** It can get you online, but check who owns the domain, whether it works on phones, and whether anyone will fix it later. A cheap site nobody can find costs more in lost enquiries.

**How long does a business website take?** Usually 4 to 6 weeks from the first call to launch for a small business site. Online stores take longer.

**Do I need a website if I have Instagram?** Instagram helps people who already follow you. A website helps people who search on Google or ask an AI tool, and it's the page your Google Business Profile links to.
