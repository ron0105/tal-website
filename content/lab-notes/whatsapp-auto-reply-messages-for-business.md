**A good WhatsApp auto-reply does three things in one or two lines: it says thanks, it says when a real person will reply, and it gives one next step, like a booking link or a short menu.** Below are 30 messages you can copy as they are, in English and Hindi, for greetings, after-hours, common questions and follow-ups. Swap the words in [brackets] for your own.

## How to set up auto-replies in the free WhatsApp Business app

Open WhatsApp Business, go to **Settings** (on iPhone) or the three-dot menu (on Android), then **Business tools**. You'll find three tools:

1. **Greeting message.** Goes out on its own when someone messages you for the first time, or after about 14 days of no messages.
2. **Away message.** Goes out on its own outside the hours you set, so nobody waits in silence at night or on a holiday.
3. **Quick replies.** Saved answers you send yourself by typing "/" in a chat. They're fast, but not automatic.

Menu names change a little between app versions, so if you don't see a label, search the settings for "greeting" or "away".

**Where the free app stops:** it can't answer a real question, book a slot or follow up a day later. For that you need the WhatsApp Business API connected to a follow-up flow. That's the part we set up for clients.

30. Hi [name], I won't keep messaging. If you need us later, just reply here and we'll pick it up. Have a great week!

## Three rules for any auto-reply

- **Say when a human will reply.** "Shortly" means nothing. "Within 10 minutes" or "by 11am tomorrow" sets the right expectation.
- **Give one next step.** A link, a menu or one question. Never a wall of text.
- **Keep the promise.** If the message says 10 minutes, reply in 10 minutes. A broken promise does more harm than no auto-reply.

## Common questions

**Does a WhatsApp auto-reply work when my phone is off?** In the free app, auto-replies depend on your phone being online. With the WhatsApp Business API, replies run from a server, so your phone can be off.

**Can I send a different auto-reply for each question?** Not in the free app. It sends the same greeting to everyone. A follow-up flow on the API can answer each question differently and hand the rest to your team.

**How many follow-ups is too many?** We stop after four messages over about a week, or as soon as someone says no. Message 30 above is a polite way to close the loop.
