**Send one link that asks a few quick questions and lets the best-fit people pick a time straight away.** Everyone else still hears back, within a day, on WhatsApp. That's what we built for ourselves in October 2026, and it's the same thing we now build for clients. Here's the before, the after, and what's still unfinished.

## The problem

When someone replied "yes, let's talk" to one of our messages, a small mess began:

- We asked what time suited them. They replied hours later.
- We offered two times. One no longer worked.
- By the time a call was booked, two or three days had gone, and some people had simply moved on.
- Every enquiry was treated the same, so a ready-to-start business owner waited in the same queue as a student asking a general question.

None of this was dramatic. It was just slow, and slow is where enquiries quietly die.

## What we built

One link: **theaddalabs.com/call**. When someone says yes, they get this one line:

> Great! Pick a time here, it takes 3 minutes.

On the page, they:

1. **Meet us.** A short hello from Rohan, so they know who they'll be talking to.
2. **Answer seven one-tap questions.** What the business does, team size, where they want help (strategy, website, posts, follow-ups), what they'd change first, when they want to start, budget, and who decides. About two minutes.
3. **Leave a WhatsApp number.**

What happens next depends on the answers:

| If the answers show... | They see |
|---|---|
| A strong fit, ready soon | Three times to pick from, booked straight into our calendar |
| A good fit, less urgent | "We'll WhatsApp you a time within a day" |
| Early days | A helpful message now, and a check-in a month later |

**Nobody is turned away.** The answers only decide how fast someone reaches Rohan.

Behind the page, every answer lands in our tracker on its own. We never retype anything, and the follow-ups are drafted for us.

## Why it works

- **One decision for the customer, not five.** Click, tap, pick a time.
- **The calendar is never shown in full.** Three offered times feel like a conversation, an open calendar feels like a form.
- **We walk into the call already knowing the business,** so the first ten minutes aren't spent on basics.

## What it's built with

Nothing exotic: the website (Next.js), Cal.com for booking, a Google Sheet as the tracker, and WhatsApp for the human part. The same pieces work for a clinic, a builder or a consultant.
